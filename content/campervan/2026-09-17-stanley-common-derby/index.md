17 June 2026

One night heading North about 5 miles to the East of Derby on a farm for £5!

The farm itself was a bit run down but the owner was out mowing our spot as we arrived, very quiet night apart from a few cows and chickens. Definitely Yorkshire views. 

Took the bikes out for a cycle round a few lanes and stopped for a beer on the way back. Lazy next morning then headed off.
